# Momo Hut Customer Analytics

## Customer Segmentation & Retention Playbook

A data-driven customer analytics project focused on Momo Hut Cafe, Basant Gaon, New Delhi.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer behaviour to identify declining engagement, segment customers, identify At-Risk customers, and develop practical CRM retention strategies.

The analysis uses an anonymised sample of 80 customer responses and applies RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis along with a stricter behavioural At-Risk rule.

## Project Objectives

- Understand customer visit behaviour
- Segment customers using RFM analysis
- Identify customers showing declining engagement
- Understand reasons behind reduced visits
- Develop targeted customer retention strategies
- Define KPIs and an implementation plan

## RFM Customer Segmentation

RFM stands for:

- **Recency** – How recently the customer visited
- **Frequency** – How often the customer visits
- **Monetary** – How much the customer spends per visit

The analysis produced five segments:

- Champions / Highly Loyal
- Loyal Customers
- Potential Loyalists
- RFM-based At-Risk Customers
- Inactive / Low Engagement

## Key Findings

The analysis identified **14 strict behavioural At-Risk customers**, representing **17.5%** of the 80-customer sample.

For this At-Risk group:

- Earlier average visits/month: **6.43**
- Current average visits/month: **1.79**
- Average decline: **4.64 visits/month**
- Approximate decline: **72.2%**
- Average satisfaction: **2.5/5**

The largest reason for reduced visits was **Product / Quality (42.9%)**, followed by **Service / Experience (28.6%)**.

## Retention Playbook

Three key CRM initiatives were recommended:

### 1. Personalised Comeback Offer
Re-engage At-Risk customers using personalised communication based on their reason for reducing visits and their preferred offer.

### 2. Loyalty Stamp Card
Build repeat-visit habits through a simple visit → stamp → reward mechanism.

### 3. Frequency Recovery Challenge
Encourage customers to rebuild their visit frequency through a structured reward journey.

## Implementation

The proposed four-week execution plan is:

**Week 1:** Identify & Segment  
**Week 2:** Re-Engage  
**Week 3:** Reward & Build Habit  
**Week 4:** Measure & Optimise
